Philly Transit Display

Flash the firmware to an ESP32 "Cheap Yellow Display" straight from this page. Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial). Plug the board in over USB, pick its variant, click Install.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS or localhost.

Not sure which board you have? The 3.5" boards say ESP32-3248S035 on the back; the 2.8" ones say ESP32-2432S028. Two USB connectors on a 2.8" board means the ST7789 variant. If touch does not respond after flashing, try the other touch variant of the same size.

After flashing

  1. The screen shows a hotspot name like TransitDisplay-29BC. Join it from your phone or laptop.
  2. A setup page opens (or browse to http://192.168.4.1). Pick your Wi-Fi and enter its password.
  3. Once connected, open http://transit-display.local to choose your routes and stops.

Source, issues, and the design notes: github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/philly-transit-display. The firmware only ever talks to SEPTA and an NTP time server; there is no account and no cloud.
